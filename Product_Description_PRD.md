# Product Requirement Document (PRD) & Product Description
**Project Name**: Remix: Inventory App (نظام إدارة المخازن والمستودعات - مكتب صحة البيئة)
**Target Organization**: Environmental Health Office - Khan Younis - Department of Infrastructure, Planning, and Development (مكتب صحة البيئة - خان يونس - دائرة البنى التحتية والتخطيط والتطوير)

---

## 1. Overview
The **Remix: Inventory App** is a highly polished, responsive, and full-featured Inventory and Warehouse Management System tailored specifically for the Environmental Health Office in Khan Younis. The application streamlines the tracking of chemical sanitizers, sterilizers, and various medical/environmental materials. It manages inventory catalogs, monitors real-time stock levels, records inbound (وارد), outbound (صادر), and consumed (مستهلك) movements, issues automated alerts for low stock or near-expiry items, maintains comprehensive audit logs, and supports cloud backups.

---

## 2. Key Pages and Features

### 2.1. Dashboard (لوحة التحكم)
* **Live Counters**: Displays absolute live counts of:
  * Total Items (الأصناف الكلية)
  * Low Stock Items (أصناف منخفضة الرصيد)
  * Near-Expiry Items (أصناف تقترب من انتهاء الصلاحية)
  * Total Transactions (إجمالي الحركات)
* **Alert Notifications**: Staggered highlights of materials requiring immediate action (e.g., materials hitting minimum safety thresholds or approaching their expiration limits).
* **Analytics**: Beautiful visual representations of inventory status, category breakdown, and monthly activity trends.

### 2.2. Materials & Items Management (الأصناف والمواد)
* **Item Properties**:
  * `id` (Unique Identifier)
  * `code` (Unique Item Code autogenerated based on dynamic category prefixes)
  * `name` (Item Name - Arabic/English)
  * `category` (e.g., Chlorine, Detergents, Equipment)
  * `unit` (e.g., Liters, Boxes, Gallons, Kilograms)
  * `manufacturer` (Production company / Manufacturer)
  * `productionDate` & `expiryDate` (Shelf-life tracking parameters)
  * `hazardLevel` (منخفض - Low | متوسط - Medium | مرتفع - High)
  * `storageLocation` (Specific shelf/bin location)
  * `currentStock` (Real-time physical quantity)
  * `minimumStock` (Safety stock margin trigger)
  * `notes` (Additional item context)
* **Actions**: Create new material, Edit existing details, Search with dynamic queries, Filter by Category or Hazard Level, and Safe Delete.

### 2.3. Transactions Registry (الحركات المخزنية)
* **Transaction Types**:
  * **وارد (Inbound / Supply)**: Increases current stock of the target material.
  * **صادر (Outbound / Issue)**: Decreases current stock of the target material.
  * **مستهلك (Consumed)**: Decreases current stock of the target material due to laboratory/field consumption.
* **Fields**:
  * `transactionNumber` (Autogenerated tracking index)
  * `date` (Transaction execution date)
  * `itemId`, `itemCode`, `itemName` (Linked material metadata)
  * `quantity` (Amount moved)
  * `stockBefore` & `stockAfter` (Stock levels captured before and after the execution)
  * `executedBy` (Employee registering the action)
  * `supplierOrReceiver` (Recipient or supplying vendor)
  * `notes` (Justification or description of the movement)
* **Validation**: Strict client-side validation preventing "Outbound" or "Consumed" quantities from exceeding the available stock balance.

### 2.4. Reports & Stocktake (التقارير والجرد)
* **Filtering Engine**: Filter database entries by Date Range, Transaction Type, Category, and Material.
* **Annual Stocktake**: Supports creating physical inventory records, compiling stock reconciliations, and generating formatted printables or spreadsheets with side-by-side computer stock vs. physical counts.

### 2.5. Audit Logs (سجل الرقابة والنشاطات)
* **Traceability Matrix**: A read-only audit log tracking every write operation on the system (adding/editing items, deleting, updating settings) along with timestamps and operator details.

### 2.6. Settings & Integration (الإعدادات والربط السحابي)
* **App Customization**: Modify Organization Name, Department, and critical threshold numbers (e.g., Expiry Warning Days).
* **Categories Ranges**: Define unique prefix codes and numeric ranges for stock number generation.
* **Cloud Integrations**:
  * **Google Cloud Firestore**: Toggle real-time database sync for secure multi-device backups.
  * **Google Drive Backup**: Secure, single-click integration that uploads two files simultaneously:
    1. A raw database snapshot (`.json`).
    2. A beautifully structured inventory status report (`.xlsx`).

## 3. End-to-End User Journeys (For Automated QA Testing)

### Flow 1: Material Onboarding & Initial Inbound
1. **Navigate** to "الأصناف والمواد" (Materials) page.
2. **Click** "إضافة مادة جديدة" (Add Material).
3. **Fill Out Form**:
   * Name: `محلول كاشف الكلور المخبري` (Chlorine Reagent)
   * Category: select Chlorine or equivalent.
   * Expiry Date: Set to exactly 1 year from today.
   * Hazard Level: `متوسط` (Medium).
   * Minimum Stock: `50`.
   * Initial Stock: `100` (this triggers an automatic initial inbound transaction).
4. **Submit Form** and verify that the item is listed on the materials page.

### Flow 2: Recording Outbound Stock (Validation Check)
1. **Navigate** to "الحركات المخزنية" (Transactions) page.
2. **Click** "تسجيل حركة جديدة" (Register Transaction).
3. **Configure Transaction**:
   * Material: Select `محلول كاشف الكلور المخبري`.
   * Type: `صادر` (Outbound).
   * Quantity: Enter `120` (which exceeds the current stock of `100`).
4. **Assert**: The system displays a validation error warning that stock is insufficient.
5. **Correct Quantity**: Change to `30`.
6. **Submit**: Verify transaction is recorded, and the material's current stock decreases to `70`.

### Flow 3: Monitoring Notifications & Expiry warnings
1. **Navigate** to Settings. Set "تنبيه اقتراب الصلاحية" (Expiry Threshold) to `30` days.
2. **Create/Edit** an item with an expiration date set to exactly `15` days from today.
3. **Navigate** to the Dashboard (لوحة التحكم).
4. **Assert**: A notification badge appears under "أصناف تقترب من انتهاء الصلاحية" with a warning pointing to that item.

### Flow 4: Exporting Data & Backups
1. **Navigate** to Settings page.
2. **Click** on "تصدير ومزامنة لـ Google Drive" (Export and Sync to Google Drive).
3. **Authorize**: Trigger standard secure OAuth flow.
4. **Assert**: The backup success notification alerts that the database and Excel file have been securely written to Google Drive.

### Flow 5: Annual Physical Stocktake & Reconciliation (الجرد الفعلي السنوي والمطابقة)
1. **Navigate** to "الأصناف والمواد" (Materials) page.
2. **Click** on "إجراء الجرد الفعلي السنوي والتسوية" (Perform Physical Stocktake & Reconciliation) button in the header.
3. **Assert**: A dialog pops up listing all materials with their System Stock (الرصيد الدفتري الحالي).
4. **Input Physical Counts**: Edit the "الرصيد الفعلي المقاس" input for any item (e.g., if System Stock is 50, enter 52 for a surplus of +2, or 47 for a deficit of -3).
5. **Add Notes**: Type general observation notes in the text field.
6. **Click** "اعتماد وتسجيل تسوية الجرد السنوي" (Commit & Record Annual Stocktake Reconciliation) button.
7. **Assert**: A success message appears, the dialog closes, and the material list automatically updates to show the new physical quantities. Inbound (وارد) or Outbound (صادر) transactions are automatically created to reconcile the difference.
